RPM vs CPM

CPM describes advertising cost per thousand impressions. RPM is a revenue-per-thousand metric used in several creator contexts. They are not interchangeable.

Why estimates vary

Audience geography, content type, advertiser demand, seasonality and platform rules can change monetization outcomes.

Sponsorship pricing

Follower count is only one input. Average views, audience relevance, deliverables, usage rights and exclusivity can materially affect a real quote.
