How it works

Use likes, comments and shares divided by views or reach. Choose one consistent denominator when tracking your own content over time. The calculator applies a simple transparent formula so you can change the assumptions and understand how the result changes.

Important context

Creator income and advertising metrics can vary substantially by audience, geography, content type, seasonality, platform rules and commercial terms. Treat this result as an estimate and compare it with your own analytics before making financial decisions.

Formula

(Likes + Comments + Shares) ÷ denominator × 100.
