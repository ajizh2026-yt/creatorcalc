How it works

Create a simple starting rate from followers, engagement and a base rate. Use it as a discussion framework rather than a fixed market price. The calculator applies a simple transparent formula so you can change the assumptions and understand how the result changes.

Important context

Creator income and advertising metrics can vary substantially by audience, geography, content type, seasonality, platform rules and commercial terms. Treat this result as an estimate and compare it with your own analytics before making financial decisions.

Formula

Followers ÷ 1,000 × base rate.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official platform calculator?

No. CreatorCalc provides independent mathematical estimates. It does not access or predict your private platform earnings.

Can I use this for planning?

Yes, as a scenario-planning tool. For important decisions, use current data from the relevant platform, advertiser or contract.
